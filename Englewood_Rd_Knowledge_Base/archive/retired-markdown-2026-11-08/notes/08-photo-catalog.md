## Mechanical room overview

**View from the entrance.** Both Polaris tanks are centered, the basement Lennox AM30 (AHU-1) is on the left, and the controls cabinet is on the right. The downstairs circulators are behind the tanks and out of view. Use this image to orient the plumbing survey. Supply and return paths still need to be traced.

The homeowner identifies the Caleffi mixer above the right Polaris tank as the domestic hot-water tempering valve. The domestic and space-heating circuits share the same water. The passive domestic recirculation return drops between the tanks, wraps around the right tank, and enters its lower Cold Inlet on the far side. The mixed outlet opposite the adjustment handle starts the domestic recirculation loop.
